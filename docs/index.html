Webnovel Emailer

Pick a novel, choose the chapters, and send them in readable email batches.

Step 1

Paste your novel link

We'll detect the title and available chapter range automatically.

Step 2

Choose what you want

The start and end chapter are both included.

Step 3

Who should receive it?

Add one or more emails. Separate them with commas or new lines.

What gets emailed?

Each email contains the chapter titles and source links for the range you selected. Chapters 30 through 400 with a batch size of 10 becomes 38 emails per recipient. The app asks for its access code once per browser session and does not copy third-party novel text.
